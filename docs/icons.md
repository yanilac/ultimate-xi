# Icons: draft list for Yani to approve

**Rule (revised):**
- An Icon is a **career-peak card** of an all-time great.
- Most Icons never played in the Premier League.
- A player who also had Premier League seasons, like Davids or Okocha, can be an Icon too. He then exists twice:
  - as his normal card for each Premier League season, such as Okocha at Bolton 2003/04;
  - as a rare gold Icon card at his career peak.
- Two copies of the same player can't be in one XI.
- The pre-1992 English legends are in.

**Ratings:** first-guess ratings run from 89 to 95, to be tuned later. **Pos** is the main position, with alternates in brackets.
